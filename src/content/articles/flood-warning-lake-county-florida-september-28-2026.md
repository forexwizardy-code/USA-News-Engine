The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Monday for Lake County, Florida, where minor flooding is occurring and minor flooding is forecast, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is occurring and minor flooding is forecast.

The NWS reported that at 8:30 a.m. EDT Monday the stage was 2.6 feet. Bankfull stage is 2.0 feet. The maximum river stage in the 24 hours ending at 8:30 a.m. EDT Monday was 2.7 feet. The river is expected to remain steady above flood stage at 2.6 feet today, and then gradually subside through Minor Flood Stage this week. Flood stage is 2.3 feet. Flood History...This crest compares to a previous crest of 2.6 feet on 10/14/1996.

The NWS says docks and boat ramps covered at South Moon Fish Camp and approaching sea wall at Blair's Jungle Den. Water approaching sites closest to the river at Fish Tales RV Resort and Astor Landing Campground. Portion of Clair Street covered by river water south of the Butler Boat Ramp. Docks underwater at the Astor Bridge Marina and along Riveredge Drive. Portions of Fox Road are covered with water nearest to the river. Acorn, Hazelnut and Redbud roads are underwater with yards inundated may occur when the river reaches 2.8 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Lake County, Florida.

The NWS said the warning applies to St Johns River near Astor.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Monday, September 28 at 9:13 a.m. EDT.

It is scheduled to expire Monday, September 28 at 11:00 p.m. EDT.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that motorists should not attempt to drive around barricades or drive cars through flooded areas.
