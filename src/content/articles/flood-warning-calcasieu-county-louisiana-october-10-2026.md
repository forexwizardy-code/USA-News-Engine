The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Saturday for Calcasieu County, Louisiana, where minor flooding is occurring and minor flooding is forecast, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is occurring and minor flooding is forecast.

The NWS reported that at 9:35 a.m. CDT Saturday the stage was 2.1 feet. The maximum river stage in the 24 hours ending at 9:35 a.m. CDT Saturday was 2.1 feet. The river will oscillate around flood stage with a maximum value of 2.1 feet this evening. Flood stage is 2.0 feet.

The NWS says minor flooding of Goos Ferry Road will occur may occur when the river reaches 2.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Calcasieu County, Louisiana.

The NWS said the warning applies to Calcasieu River near White Oak Park.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Saturday, October 10 at 9:58 a.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Tuesday, October 13 at 1:00 p.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that motorists should not attempt to drive around barricades or drive cars through flooded areas.
