The National Weather Service issued a Flash Flood Warning Tuesday for Yavapai County, Arizona, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The full details are provided in the official NWS alert.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Yavapai County, Arizona.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Tuesday, September 29 at 5:48 a.m. MST.

It remains in effect until Tuesday, September 29 at 8:45 a.m. MST, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that flooding of washes and creeks will occur, some dirt roads will become muddy and impassable.
