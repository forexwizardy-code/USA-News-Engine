The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Sunday for Calhoun County, Illinois, where minor flooding is occurring, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is occurring.

The NWS reported that at 1:00 p.m. CDT Sunday the stage was 25.1 feet. The maximum river stage in the 24 hours ending at 1:00 p.m. CDT Sunday was 25.5 feet. The river is expected to fall below flood stage this afternoon and continue falling to 18.5 feet Sunday, October 04. Flood stage is 25.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Calhoun County, Illinois.

The NWS said the warning applies to Mississippi River at Clarksville.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Sunday, September 27 at 1:24 p.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Monday, September 28 at 8:23 p.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that motorists should not attempt to drive around barricades or drive cars through flooded areas.
