Saudi-Led Airstrikes in Yemen Kill More Than 60 in Prison, 
Rebels Say

SANA, Yemen — Airstrikes by the Saudi-led coalition fighting Houthi rebels in Yemen hit a university used as a detention center in a southwestern province on Sunday, killing at least 60 people, officials and the rebels’ health ministry said.  A spokesman for the International Committee of the Red Cross said the death toll could be more than 100.  The attack was the deadliest so far this year by the coalition, which has faced international criticism for airstrikes that have killed civilians and hit nonmilitary targets."
