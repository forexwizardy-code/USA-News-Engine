The National Weather Service issued a Severe Thunderstorm Warning Sunday for Alamosa County, Colorado, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The full details are provided in the official NWS alert.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Alamosa County, Colorado.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Sunday, October 11 at 6:30 p.m. MDT.

It remains in effect until Sunday, October 11 at 7:00 p.m. MDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that for your protection move to an interior room on the lowest floor of a building.
