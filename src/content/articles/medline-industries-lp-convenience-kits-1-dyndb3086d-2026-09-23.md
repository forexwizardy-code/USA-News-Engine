Medline Industries, LP is recalling Convenience Kits: 1) DYNDB3086D because of a contamination risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 11, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Medline Convenience Kits: 1) DYNDB3086D, Catalog Number: DYNDB3086D; 2) DYNDB3100, Catalog Number: DYNDB3100; 3) DYNDH1619A, Catalog Number: DYNDH1619A; 4) DYNDH1620A, Catalog Number: DYNDH1620A;


## Why it is being recalled
The kits contain certain lots of sterile applicators which may contain fungal contamination that may occur under certain environmental conditions, allowing the growth of Aspergillus penicillioides. Contamination of skin preparation products with Aspergillus penicillioides may lead to serious adverse health consequences, including systemic infection, sepsis, illness, and death.


## Where it was sold or distributed
US Nationwide distribution.

Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
