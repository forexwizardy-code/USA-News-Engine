The National Weather Service issued a Severe Thunderstorm Warning Thursday for Bee County, Texas, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The full details are provided in the official NWS alert.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Bee County, Texas.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Thursday, October 1 at 7:21 p.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Thursday, October 1 at 8:15 p.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that anyone outdoors should move to shelter inside a well-built structure and stay away from windows.
