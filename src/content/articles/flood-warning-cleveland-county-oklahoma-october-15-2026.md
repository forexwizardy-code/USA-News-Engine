The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Thursday for Cleveland County, Oklahoma, where minor flooding is forecast, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is forecast.

The NWS reported that at 2:30 p.m. CDT Thursday the stage was 8.6 feet. Bankfull stage is 12.0 feet. The river is expected to rise above flood stage tomorrow evening to a crest of 12.6 feet Saturday morning. It will then fall below flood stage early Sunday morning. Flood stage is 12.0 feet. Flood History...This crest compares to a previous crest of 12.5 feet on 05/08/2015.

The NWS says flooding to depths of a foot occurs over areas along and near the river on the Cleveland-McClain County border from near Noble... to areas south of Purcell and Lexington may occur when the river reaches 13.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Cleveland County, Oklahoma.

The NWS said the warning applies to Canadian River at Purcell.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Thursday, October 15 at 2:57 p.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Sunday, October 18 at 9:00 a.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads.
