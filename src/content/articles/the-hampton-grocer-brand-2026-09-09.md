THE HAMPTON GROCER is recalling brand because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 14, 2026.


## What is being recalled
THE HAMPTON GROCER brand; Lacnola; SMALL BATCH LACTATION GRANOLA, 8oz, stand up pouches; NUTRIENT DENSE INGREDIENTS + MORINGA; SUPPORTS + PROMOTES HEALTHY LACTATION; INGREDIENTS Organic Gluten Free Oa


## Why it is being recalled
Product may be contaminated with Salmonella.


## Where it was sold or distributed
Nationwide


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
