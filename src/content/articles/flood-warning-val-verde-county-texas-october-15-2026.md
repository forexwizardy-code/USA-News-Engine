The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Thursday for Val Verde County, Texas, where moderate flooding is forecast, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that moderate flooding is forecast.

The NWS reported that at 8:15 a.m. CDT Thursday the stage was 1.5 feet. Bankfull stage is 14.0 feet. The river will rise above flood stage late tomorrow morning to 17.6 feet early Saturday morning. It will then fall below flood stage early Sunday afternoon. Flood stage is 14.0 feet.

The NWS says (4.3 meters), minor lowland flooding into low areas of the flood plain causes no significant damage. At 15.0 feet, (4.6 meters), moderate lowland flooding into the right flood plain floods campsites below Dryden to Amistad Reservoir. Turbulence is very dangerous for swimmers, tubers and canoeists. Livestock are trapped in low areas of the channel and banks and potentially can drown. At 18.0 feet, (5.5 meters), the flow is well into the flood plain and threatens campers, gear and autos below Dryden to Amistad Reservoir. The flow is very turbulent with eddies and whirlpools dangerous to swimmers, canoeists and kayakers from above Foster Ranch to Amistad Reservoir may occur when the river reaches 14.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Val Verde County, Texas.

The NWS said the warning applies to Rio Grande at Foster Ranch.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Thursday, October 15 at 8:58 a.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Monday, October 19 at 1:00 a.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that even 6 inches of fast-moving flood water can knock you off your feet and a depth of 2 feet will float your car.
