A national supplier is recalling more than **two dozen frozen vegetable products** sold under several brand names across 19 states after routine testing detected listeria bacteria at one of its processing plants.

The recall covers bags of frozen broccoli, corn, peas, and mixed blends with best-by dates extending into next year. No illnesses have been reported in connection with the products, the company said.

## What is recalled

The affected items were distributed to major grocery chains and discount clubs. Consumers can identify them by:

- The **brand name** and product description printed on the front.
- A **lot code** and best-by date printed on the back or bottom seam.
- A **plant code** matching the facility identified in the recall notice.

A full list of products, lot codes, and photos is available on the company's recall page and through the Food and Drug Administration's recall database.

## What to do

Health officials advised consumers who purchased the affected products to **throw them away or return them** to the place of purchase for a refund. Surfaces and containers that touched the product should be washed thoroughly.

Listeria can cause serious and sometimes fatal infections in young children, older adults, and people with weakened immune systems.

> "Listeria is unusual because it can grow at freezer temperatures, which is why a recall like this is taken seriously even without confirmed illnesses," a food safety specialist said. "When in doubt, throw it out."

The company said it has suspended production at the affected line while investigators determine the source of the contamination.
