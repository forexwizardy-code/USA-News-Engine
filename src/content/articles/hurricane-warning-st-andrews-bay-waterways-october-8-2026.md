The National Weather Service issued a Hurricane Warning Thursday for St. Andrews Bay Waterways, where north winds 40 to 50 kt with gusts up to 70 kt and seas 16 to 21 ft, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that north winds 40 to 50 kt with gusts up to 70 kt and seas 16 to 21 ft.

The NWS notes potential impacts: extremely strong winds will cause hazardous seas which will likely capsize or damage vessels and severely reduce visibility.


## Areas affected
The alert covers St. Andrews Bay Waterways.

The NWS said the warning applies to St. Andrews Bay Waterways, Coastal waters from Mexico Beach to Okaloosa Walton County Line FL out 20 NM, Waters from Mexico Beach to Okaloosa Walton County Line FL from 20 to 60 NM and Waters from Apalachicola to Mexico Beach FL from 20 to 60 NM.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Thursday, October 8 at 5:23 p.m. EDT.

It is scheduled to expire Friday, October 9 at 1:30 a.m. EDT.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that mariners should remain in port, alter course, and/or secure the vessel for extreme conditions.
