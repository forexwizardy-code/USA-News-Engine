Lexunder Inc is recalling Food to Live Organic SuperGrass Powder because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 7, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Food to Live Organic SuperGrass Powder; 6 oz, 12 oz, 1 lb, 4 lb


## Why it is being recalled
Contaminated with Salmonella


## Where it was sold or distributed
AK, AR, AZ, CA, CO, CT, DE, FL, GA, HI, IA, IL, IN, KY, LA, MA, MD, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, NE, NH, NJ, NV, NY, OH, OK, OR, PA, RI, SC, TN, TX, UT, VA, VI, WA, WI, WV, and U.S. Virgin Islands.


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
