The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Saturday for Carroll County, Missouri, where minor flooding is forecast, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is forecast.

The NWS reported that there is no current observed data. The river is expected to rise to a crest of 24.2 feet early tomorrow afternoon. It will then fall below flood stage Monday afternoon. Flood stage is 19.0 feet.

The NWS says flooding of low-lying areas occurs along the west bank of the Grand River near the U.S. Highway 24 bridge about 1 mile west of Brunswick. At 22.0 feet, Low-lying areas along the east bank of the Grand River near the U.S. Highway 24 bridge begin to flood may occur when the river reaches 19.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Carroll County, Missouri.

The NWS said the warning applies to Grand River near Brunswick.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Saturday, October 3 at 7:58 p.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Tuesday, October 6 at 5:00 p.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that motorists should not attempt to drive around barricades or drive cars through flooded areas.
