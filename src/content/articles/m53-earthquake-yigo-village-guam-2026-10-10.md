A magnitude 5.3 earthquake struck Yigo Village, Guam on Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 3:58 a.m. UTC, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.


## What USGS reported
The U.S. Geological Survey recorded the earthquake at magnitude 5.3 at a depth of 111.6 kilometers centered near 41 km NNE of Yigo Village, Guam.

USGS listed the event status as &quot;reviewed,&quot; indicating that a seismologist had reviewed the automated solution.

USGS assigned the event a significance score of 444.


## Magnitude and depth
The earthquake had a magnitude of 5.3 on the body-wave magnitude (Mb) scale.

The hypocenter was located at a depth of 111.6 kilometers.


## Where the earthquake occurred
The epicenter was located near 41 km NNE of Yigo Village, Guam (Guam).

USGS listed the coordinates as 13.8594°N, 145.0892°E.


## Felt reports
As of the most recent USGS update, 30 &quot;Did You Feel It?&quot; reports were submitted by residents near the epicenter.

USGS computed a Community Determined Intensity (CDI) of 4.1 from those reports.
