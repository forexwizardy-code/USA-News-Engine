The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Monday for Lake County, Illinois, where minor flooding is occurring, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is occurring.

The NWS reported that at 7:30 a.m. CDT Monday the stage was 7.1 feet. The maximum river stage in the 24 hours ending at 7:30 a.m. CDT Monday was 7.6 feet. The river is expected to fall below flood stage by early this evening. Flood stage is 7.0 feet.

The NWS says forest land inundated near the river in Gurnee may occur when the river reaches 7.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Lake County, Illinois.

The NWS said the warning applies to Des Plaines River from US-41 in Gurnee downstream to Townline Road in Vernon Hills, including the Gurnee gauge.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Monday, September 28 at 8:37 a.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Monday, September 28 at 7:00 p.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that persons along rivers and streams in the warned area should take immediate precautions to protect life and property. The agency said a Flood Warning means water levels above flood stage are imminent or may already be occurring.
