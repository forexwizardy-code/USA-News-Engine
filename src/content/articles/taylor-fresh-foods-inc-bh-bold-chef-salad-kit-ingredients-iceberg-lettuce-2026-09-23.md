Taylor Fresh Foods Inc is recalling BH Bold Chef Salad Kit. Ingredients: Iceberg Lettuce because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 7, 2026.


## What is being recalled
BH Bold Chef Salad Kit. Ingredients: Iceberg Lettuce, Pico De Gallo (***Jalapeno Pepper), Jalapeno Ranch Dressing (***Jalapeno Peppers***), Egg, Cucumber, Black Olives *** Carrot, Red Cabbage.


## Why it is being recalled
Potential contamination with Salmonella.


## Where it was sold or distributed
Domestic: AL, AR, FL, GA, IL, IN, KS, KY, LA, MA, ME, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, NE, NH, NY, OH, OK, SC, TN, TX, VT, WI, WV. Foreign: Not applicable.


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
