A magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck Lake Minchumina, Alaska on Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 11:07 a.m. UTC, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.


## What USGS reported
The U.S. Geological Survey recorded the earthquake at magnitude 5.6 at a depth of 6.3 kilometers centered near 43 km NNW of Lake Minchumina, Alaska.

USGS listed the event status as &quot;automatic,&quot; meaning the solution had not yet been reviewed by a seismologist at the time of publication.

USGS assigned the event a significance score of 482.


## Magnitude and depth
The earthquake had a magnitude of 5.6 on the local magnitude (ML) scale.

The hypocenter was located at a depth of 6.3 kilometers, classified by USGS as a shallow event.


## Where the earthquake occurred
The epicenter was located near 43 km NNW of Lake Minchumina, Alaska.

USGS listed the coordinates as 64.2240°N, 152.7380°W.
