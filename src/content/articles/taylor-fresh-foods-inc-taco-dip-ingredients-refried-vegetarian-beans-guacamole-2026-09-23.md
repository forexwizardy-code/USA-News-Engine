Taylor Fresh Foods Inc is recalling Taco Dip. Ingredients: Refried Vegetarian Beans *** Guacamole (***Pico De Gallo [***Jalapeno Pepper]***) because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 7, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Taco Dip. Ingredients: Refried Vegetarian Beans *** Guacamole (***Pico De Gallo [***Jalapeno Pepper]***), Pico De Gallo (***Jalapeno Pepper), Mild Cheddar Cheese *** Sour Cream *** Black Ripe Olives *


## Why it is being recalled
Potential contamination with Salmonella.


## Where it was sold or distributed
Domestic: AL, AR, FL, GA, IL, IN, KS, KY, LA, MA, ME, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, NE, NH, NY, OH, OK, SC, TN, TX, VT, WI, WV. Foreign: Not applicable.


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
