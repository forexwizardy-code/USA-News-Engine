A magnitude 4.2 earthquake struck Wauna, Washington on Tuesday, September 29, 2026 at 9:05 p.m. UTC, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.


## What USGS reported
The U.S. Geological Survey recorded the earthquake at magnitude 4.2 at a depth of 27.9 kilometers centered near 6 km WNW of Wauna, Washington.

USGS listed the event status as &quot;reviewed,&quot; indicating that a seismologist had reviewed the automated solution.

USGS assigned the event a significance score of 645.


## Magnitude and depth
The earthquake had a magnitude of 4.2 on the local magnitude (ML) scale.

The hypocenter was located at a depth of 27.9 kilometers, within the shallow-to-intermediate range typical of crustal activity.


## Where the earthquake occurred
The epicenter was located near 6 km WNW of Wauna, Washington.

USGS listed the coordinates as 47.4003°N, 122.7220°W.


## Felt reports
As of the most recent USGS update, 9,284 &quot;Did You Feel It?&quot; reports were submitted by residents near the epicenter.

USGS computed a Community Determined Intensity (CDI) of 3.8 from those reports.

USGS estimated a peak Modified Mercalli Intensity (MMI) of 3.5 for the event.


## USGS alert and tsunami information
USGS assigned a green (little to no impact expected) PAGER alert level for the earthquake through its Prompt Assessment of Global Earthquakes for Response (PAGER) system.
