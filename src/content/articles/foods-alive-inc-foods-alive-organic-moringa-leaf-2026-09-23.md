FOODS ALIVE INC. is recalling Foods Alive organic moringa leaf powder because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 26, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Foods Alive organic moringa leaf powder, 8oz pouch, UPC 8 91551 00092 8


## Why it is being recalled
Potential Salmonella contamination


## Where it was sold or distributed
Nationwide US and UAE. The recalled organic moringa leaf powder was distributed nationwide in retail stores and through online sellers including but not limited to www.foodsalive.com.


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
