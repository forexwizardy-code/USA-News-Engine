Frutas y Hortalizas del Sur S.A. is recalling Organic Triple Berry Blend Strawberries because of a e. coli risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 28, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Organic Triple Berry Blend Strawberries, Blackberries &amp; Blueberries, NET WT 10 OZ (283g), packed in plastic bag, 5 bags per case of 10 OZ each. KEEP FROZEN


## Why it is being recalled
Potential E. coli O145:H28 Contamination


## Where it was sold or distributed
AR


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
