International Sprout Holdings, Inc is recalling alfalfa because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 23, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Alfalfa, Net Weight 50 lbs. bag, Product of USA, International Specialty Supply 1011 Volunteer Dr. Cookeville, TN 38506


## Why it is being recalled
Potential E. coli and Salmonella contamination


## Where it was sold or distributed
Wholesale consignees in 16 states: CA, FL, HI, IL, MA, MN, MO, MT, NY, OH, OR, PA, TN, SC, TX, UT; Puerto Rico; Canada, Mexico, Tahiti, and Venezuela


## What consumers should do
Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
