A magnitude 5.5 earthquake struck Aleneva, Alaska on Tuesday, October 6, 2026 at 6:34 p.m. UTC, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.


## What USGS reported
The U.S. Geological Survey recorded the earthquake at magnitude 5.5 at a depth of 87.6 kilometers centered near 92 km NNW of Aleneva, Alaska.

USGS listed the event status as &quot;reviewed,&quot; indicating that a seismologist had reviewed the automated solution.

USGS assigned the event a significance score of 579.


## Magnitude and depth
The earthquake had a magnitude of 5.5 on the local magnitude (ML) scale.

The hypocenter was located at a depth of 87.6 kilometers.


## Where the earthquake occurred
The epicenter was located near 92 km NNW of Aleneva, Alaska.

USGS listed the coordinates as 58.8020°N, 153.6090°W.


## Felt reports
As of the most recent USGS update, 265 &quot;Did You Feel It?&quot; reports were submitted by residents near the epicenter.

USGS computed a Community Determined Intensity (CDI) of 4.3 from those reports.

USGS estimated a peak Modified Mercalli Intensity (MMI) of 4.2 for the event.


## USGS alert and tsunami information
USGS assigned a green (little to no impact expected) PAGER alert level for the earthquake through its Prompt Assessment of Global Earthquakes for Response (PAGER) system.
