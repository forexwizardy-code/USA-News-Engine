A magnitude 5.0 earthquake struck Saipan, Northern Mariana Islands on Monday, October 5, 2026 at 10:52 a.m. UTC, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.


## What USGS reported
The U.S. Geological Survey recorded the earthquake at magnitude 5.0 at a depth of 10.0 kilometers centered near 187 km ENE of Saipan, Northern Mariana Islands.

USGS listed the event status as &quot;reviewed,&quot; indicating that a seismologist had reviewed the automated solution.

USGS assigned the event a significance score of 385.


## Magnitude and depth
The earthquake had a magnitude of 5.0 on the body-wave magnitude (Mb) scale.

The hypocenter was located at a depth of 10.0 kilometers, within the shallow-to-intermediate range typical of crustal activity.


## Where the earthquake occurred
The epicenter was located near 187 km ENE of Saipan, Northern Mariana Islands (Northern Mariana Islands).

USGS listed the coordinates as 15.8046°N, 147.3950°E.
