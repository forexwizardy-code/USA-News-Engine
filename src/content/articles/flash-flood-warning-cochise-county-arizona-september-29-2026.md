The National Weather Service issued a Flash Flood Warning Tuesday for Cochise County, Arizona, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The full details are provided in the official NWS alert.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Cochise County, Arizona.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Tuesday, September 29 at 4:56 p.m. MST.

It remains in effect until Tuesday, September 29 at 7:15 p.m. MST, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads.
