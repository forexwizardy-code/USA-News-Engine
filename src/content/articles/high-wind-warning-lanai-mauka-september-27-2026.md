The National Weather Service issued a High Wind Warning Sunday for Lanai Mauka, where east winds 30 to 45 mph with gusts up to 75 mph, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that east winds 30 to 45 mph with gusts up to 75 mph.

The NWS notes potential impacts: damaging winds may blow down trees and power lines and damage roofs. Power outages are possible. Travel will be difficult, especially for high profile vehicles.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Lanai Mauka.

The NWS said the warning applies to Portions of Maui, Big Island, Molokai, and Lanai over and downwind of terrain, especially in west Maui and Kohala.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Sunday, September 27 at 5:20 a.m. HST.

It remains in effect until Sunday, September 27 at 6:00 p.m. HST, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that loose outdoor items should be brought inside or secured properly. The agency said a High Wind Warning means that winds are expected to be at least 40 mph, with gusts over 60 mph.
