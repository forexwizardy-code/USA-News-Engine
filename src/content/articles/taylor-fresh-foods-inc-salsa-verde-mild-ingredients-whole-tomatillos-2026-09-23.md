Taylor Fresh Foods Inc is recalling Salsa Verde Mild. Ingredients: Whole Tomatillos (***) because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The recall was initiated on August 7, 2026.


## What is being recalled
Salsa Verde Mild. Ingredients: Whole Tomatillos (***), Roasted Poblano Chile, Jalapeno Pepper, Cilantro, Serrano Pepper, Garlic, Salt. Net Wt: 12 oz. Keep Refrigerated. Distributed By: Taylor Farms I


## Why it is being recalled
Potential contamination with Salmonella.


## Where it was sold or distributed
Domestic: AL, AR, FL, GA, IL, IN, KS, KY, LA, MA, ME, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, NE, NH, NY, OH, OK, SC, TN, TX, VT, WI, WV. Foreign: Not applicable.

Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
