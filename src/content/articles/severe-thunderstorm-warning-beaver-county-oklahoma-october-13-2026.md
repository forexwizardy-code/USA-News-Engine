The full details are provided in the official NWS alert.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Beaver County, Oklahoma.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Tuesday, October 13 at 7:50 p.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Tuesday, October 13 at 8:30 p.m. CDT, the NWS said.
