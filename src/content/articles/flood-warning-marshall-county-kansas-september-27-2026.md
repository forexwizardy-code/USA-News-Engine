The National Weather Service issued a Flood Warning Sunday for Marshall County, Kansas, where minor flooding is forecast, according to the agency's alert.


## What the warning says
The NWS alert states that minor flooding is forecast.

The NWS reported that at 2:15 p.m. CDT Sunday the stage was 25.9 feet. The river is expected to rise above flood stage late this afternoon to a crest of 26.1 feet this evening. It will then fall below flood stage late this evening. Flood stage is 26.0 feet.

The NWS says minor lowland flooding occurs from Marysville to Tuttle Creek Lake may occur when the river reaches 26.0 feet.


## Areas affected
The alert covers Marshall County, Kansas.

The NWS said the warning applies to Big Blue River near Blue Rapids.


## How long the warning is in effect
The warning took effect Sunday, September 27 at 2:37 p.m. CDT.

It remains in effect until Monday, September 28 at 10:00 a.m. CDT, the NWS said.


## Safety information
The National Weather Service advises that motorists should not attempt to drive around barricades or drive cars through flooded areas.
