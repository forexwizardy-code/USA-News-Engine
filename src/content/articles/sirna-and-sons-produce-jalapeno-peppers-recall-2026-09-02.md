Sirna and Sons Produce, Inc is recalling jalapeno peppers because of a salmonella risk, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The FDA reported the recall on September 2, 2026. The recall was initiated on August 5, 2026.

## What is being recalled

The recall involves Sirna & Sons Produce PEPPER JALAPENO BREAK 1LB, repackaged into 1 lb. plastic bags. Product Number: 55255. The product was packed by Sirna & Sons Produce, 7176 State Route 88, Ravenna, OH 44266, and distributed for food service use.

## Why it is being recalled

The recall was initiated due to potential Salmonella Javiana contamination, according to the FDA.

## Where it was distributed

The product was distributed to Ohio and Pennsylvania, for food service use only.

## What consumers should do

Consumers can review the FDA recall record for product and distribution details.
